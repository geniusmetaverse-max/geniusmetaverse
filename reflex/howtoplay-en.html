How to Play

Complete Guide for RefleX Game Modes

Color Switch Tap

Tap the correct button the exact moment the on-screen color matches the target color. Perfect your timing!

Falling Block Dodge

Swipe left or right to avoid the obstacles falling from the top. Trust your reflexes!

Light

React as quickly as possible to the light signals appearing on the screen. Don't blink!

Tap at the Beat

Tap the screen in perfect synchronization with the background rhythm. Don't miss a beat!

Quick Reaction Quiz

Decide whether the statement on the screen is true or false in just seconds. Think fast!

Rapid Tap

Tap the screen as many times as you can within the given time limit. Speed up those fingers!

Survival Rush

Survive as long as you can through increasingly faster and more difficult obstacles.

Blink Challenge

Follow the blinking targets and test your speed by reacting at the exact right time.

Chain Reaction

Maintain the combo by completing on-screen chain reactions with precise timing.

Dual Tap Reflex

Use both hands to quickly tap targets appearing simultaneously on different sides of the screen.

Mirror Tap

Challenge your brain by applying the exact symmetrical (mirrored) movement of what appears on screen.

Mental Math

Solve fast-paced arithmetic math problems in your head within seconds.

Nitro Rush PRO

Push the speed limits! Dodge and weave through obstacles approaching at a crazy pace.

Split Focus PRO

Push your multitasking boundaries by managing two different tasks at the exact same time.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is RefleX? +
RefleX is a modern skill game designed to improve your reflexes, hand-eye coordination, and mental focus. With a total of 14 different game modes across Visual, Auditory, and Mental categories, it allows you to test your limits.
How do I unlock game modes? +
When you first start the game, the "Core" game modes at Level 1 are unlocked. As you play, you earn Experience Points (XP) and level up. When you reach specific levels (e.g., Level 16, 31, 46, etc.), you can use your earned in-game points to unlock new and more challenging "Extra" game modes.
Which language options are available? +
You can play RefleX in both Turkish and English by making your selection from the "Settings > Language" menu. You can change your language preference at any time within the game.
What do the power-ups (Support Powers) in the Store do? +
You can purchase various power-ups from the Store using the Points you earn by playing. These power-ups give you an advantage in challenging game modes and help you achieve higher scores to climb the leaderboards. If you run out of points, you can also get additional point packs from the store.
What are the benefits of a RefleX PRO subscription? +
With the RefleX PRO package, you get the following privileges that take your gaming experience to the next level:

- Exclusive Game Modes: Access to the most challenging game modes like "Nitro Rush" and "Split Focus", which are only available to PRO members.
- 2x Rewards: You earn twice as many (2x) Points and XP from every game you play.
- Ad-Free Experience: All ads are completely removed, allowing you to enjoy uninterrupted gameplay.
- PRO Badge: A stylish, exclusive "PRO" badge is displayed right next to your name on the leaderboards.
Can I cancel my subscription after purchasing? +
- One-time point packs are credited to your account instantly and cannot be refunded or canceled.
- You can cancel your RefleX PRO Monthly Subscription at any time through your App Store or Google Play Store account settings and turn off auto-renewal. If you cancel, you will continue to enjoy all your PRO benefits until the end of your billing cycle.

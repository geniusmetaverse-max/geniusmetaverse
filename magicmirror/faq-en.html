Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does Magic Mirror work?

Magic Mirror analyzes the photo you upload with AI and generates a personalized “virtual try-on” image based on the wedding dress style you choose. This way, you can preview different designs and see how they look on you before making a decision.

2. What should I consider when uploading a photo?

In line with our ethical standards and respect for user privacy, photos of children, animals, partial body images (such as only a hand, arm, or leg), or object photos are not accepted. These rules ensure a safe and respectful experience for everyone. Our AI service is designed to process photos where the full body or face is clearly visible, providing the most accurate and elegant results.

3. Are my photos secure?

Absolutely. Your uploaded photos are used solely for image generation and are stored securely. They are never shared with third parties.

4. Can I save my generated images?

Yes. All generated images can be saved to your phone without watermarks.

5. Can I share my images with others?

Yes. You can share your generated images directly from your phone via social media or messaging apps.

6. Do I need to log in with my Apple/Google account to purchase any try-on package?

No. Logging in with your Google/Apple account securely links your data and credits to your account. Even if you delete the app, change your device, or clear your data, all your rights remain intact. A single login keeps your trials and packages safe and accessible at all times.

7. Do I have a free trial?

Yes! Every user receives one free credit as a welcome gift.

8. How can I continue after using my free credit?

Once your free credit is used, you can continue creating stunning try-ons by purchasing one of the try-on packages that best suits your needs.

9. What is the validity period of purchased packages?

All purchased packages and credits are unlimited in validity. You can use them anytime you wish.

10. Can I cancel a purchased package?

Purchased packages are one-time and non-refundable.

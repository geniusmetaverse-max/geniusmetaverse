Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is WordLegend? +
WordLegend is a modern word guessing game designed to improve your vocabulary and entertain you. Every day you can play with new words ranging from 3 to 6 letters in different game modes.
What are the game modes? +
Experience different game modes developed to make word guessing more fun and engaging:

Daily Challenges: A 6-letter word game playable only once per day. Play without spending energy and earn points to climb the leaderboard.

Standard Mode: Find the word within a set time and 5 guesses! Level up and earn points!
Hard Mode: Find the word within a set time and only one guess! Level up and earn points!
Timeless Mode: Find the word with 5 guesses and no time limit! No level progression or point rewards in this mode.
Unlimited Mode: Find the word with no time or guess limits! No level progression or point rewards in this mode.
Which language options are available? +
You can play WordLegend in both Turkish and English by selecting your preference in Settings/Language. You can change your language preference anytime.

For both languages, some words may be excluded from the word pool due to ethical or technical reasons.
Is there a daily word limit? +
No. You can solve as many words as you like each day.

The “Daily Challenges” mode can only be played once per day with a word selected specifically for that day.
How are leaderboards calculated? +
All rankings and results on the Stats page are calculated based on the number of games played and the number of correctly guessed words, regardless of word length or game mode (Daily Challenges, Standard, Hard).
What does WordLegend Pro provide? +
With WordLegend Pro you gain access to exclusive benefits:

- 20 free Energy refills every day
- 5,000 free Points every month
- Unlock the “My Words” page to access personalized results and your dictionary based on your game performance
- A special “Pro” tag in the leaderboards
Can I cancel purchased packages? +
- Mini, Standard, Mega, and Ultra point packages are one-time purchases and cannot be canceled.
- You can cancel your WordLegend Pro Monthly Subscription anytime and stop automatic renewal. You will retain your benefits until the last day of your subscription period.

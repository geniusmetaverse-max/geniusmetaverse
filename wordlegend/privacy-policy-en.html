1. Introduction

We value your privacy at WordLegend. WordLegend ("Application") is a mobile word game designed to improve your vocabulary. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is collected, used, protected, and shared while using the application. By using the application, you acknowledge that you have read and accepted this Privacy Policy.

2. Data Collected

Personal Data

  • When signing in with Firebase Authentication: User ID (UID), email address (optional)
  • Game progress: Level, number of solved words, streak, badges
  • Played words (to avoid repetition)

Automatically Collected Data

  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • Application usage statistics (Analytics)

3. Use of Data

  • Ensure smooth operation of the game.
  • Save your progress and badges.
  • Provide a personalized gaming experience.
  • Develop in-app improvements and new features.
  • Fulfill legal obligations.
  • Provide support and communication.

4. Data Sharing and Transfers

  • Your data is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Limited sharing is only with technical service providers (Google Cloud, Firebase, RevenueCat).
  • International transfers: Data may be processed on Google servers in the USA. These transfers are protected under GDPR Standard Contractual Clauses and other approved mechanisms.
  • Data may be disclosed if legally required (court order, regulatory request).

5. Data Retention Periods

  • Usage/analytics data: Retained up to 24 months (longer if anonymized).
  • Payment metadata: 10 years (for tax compliance).
  • Support requests: Retained for 1 year after resolution.

6. Data Security

Technical and administrative measures such as encryption, access control, and regular security testing are applied in line with industry standards. However, it is important to note that no transmission over the internet is completely risk-free.

7. User Rights (GDPR Articles 15–22)

  • Right to access and request information about your personal data.
  • Right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to request deletion or anonymization of your data (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Right to object to or request restriction of processing.
  • Right to data portability (receive your data in a structured format).
  • Right to object to automated decision-making (including AI processing where applicable).
